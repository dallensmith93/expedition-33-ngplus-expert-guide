# BOSS — Simon

**Type:** Superboss  
**Mechanics:**  
- Kills that are not instantly revived can be **erased** (cannot revive).  
- Steals shields off-turn.  
- Chroma gather = that character set to **1 HP** (unavoidable).  
- Phase 2: every sword swing is **two hits**.  
- Below half: **deletes the current trio** even at full HP. Backup team comes in.  

**Recommended Party:** Maelle (Cheater, First Strike, Medalum, Gommage), Verso, Lune. Reserve: Sciel + Monoco built to finish if phase-2 wipe happens.  
**Recommended Pictos/Luminas:** Second Chance, Auto-revive effects, Cheater, Painted Power, SOS.  
**Reward:** Colour of Lumina x50, Perfect catalyst x2, **Simoso** (Verso), Simon trophy.  
**NG+ Expert Strategy:** Superboss Build Recommended. Hold Gommage until he is just above half in phase 2, mark/burn, Virtuose Gommage. Killing him before the trio-delete is the clean line.
