# Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 — Part 1 — Lumière: The Gommage

## EPISODE GOAL

Clear every finite Prologue pickup, secret, and Festival Token reward before the expedition launches. On NG+ Expert this zone is not a threat. The missables are.

## STARTING POINT

Lumière rooftops. Gustave. Opening control after the first cinematic.

## THIS SESSION

1. Confirm Expert. Rooftops: flower for Sophie, follow Maelle, win or skip the tutorial fight.
2. In the plaza, do every secret **before** you walk Sophie to the harbour: Richard, Eloise, Nicolas, Trash-can Man, Life Enjoyers (33 knocks), Mime.
3. After the Gommage, Festival: Antoine quiz + Maelle dock duel for three tokens.
4. Spend tokens on **Old Key** (Colette) and **Weird Pictos** (Tom). Buy the Bun only if you do not already own it.
5. Talk to Emma and launch. Stop. Spring Meadows is Episode 2.

## BEFORE WE START

- Difficulty: **Expert**. Confirm it in Gameplay before the first fight.
- If you own Lumina Sets (1.5.0), make a set named `NG+ Carry` with Cheater + Painted Power + First Strike on your damage dealer. You cannot equip most of this until the party exists — just have the plan.
- You cannot use Colour of Lumina or upgrade weapons yet. Pick them up anyway.

# ⚠ MISSABLE

**Old Key** — Colette at the Festival, one Festival Token. Used in Episode 14 for the Fracture Survivor journal. Patch 1.5.0 added a second Key in the finale Lumière area. Buy it here anyway.

**Lumière Mime** — record *Lumière* + achievement **A Peculiar Encounter**. The record can be recovered in Episode 30. The achievement cannot.

**Trash-can Man** — interact with the shiny-rim trash can until he appears. Episode 30's Perfect Chroma Catalyst conversation only exists if you found him here.

**Weird Pictos** — Tom at the Festival. Required later in Gestral Village.

**Life Enjoyers / Nicolas / Eloise / Richard→Jules** — finite Prologue rewards. After you walk Sophie to the harbour and confirm, this district is gone.

## WALKTHROUGH

### FROM: Opening rooftop

☐ Turn right. Interact with the bench.

☐ Take **A Flower for Sophie**. Choose Yes.

☐ Follow Maelle across the rooftops. Hold sprint. Use the grapple when she points at it.

☐ There is nothing to collect on these roofs. Do not drop off.

### Combat tutorial — Maelle

☐ When Maelle challenges you, take the tutorial if it is offered. On NG+ you can skip the teaching prompts; she then fights for real.

# BOSS — Maelle (tutorial / rooftop duel)

**Type:** Story  
**Weakness:** none that matters  
**Important Mechanics:** Overcharge at 10. Lumière Assault builds the gauge.  
**Dangerous Attacks:** single lunge after "Maelle attacks"; 3-hit stab / rise / spin.  
**Parry/Dodge Notes:** parry the single lunge on her grunt. Dodge the 3-hit if Expert timing feels tight.  
**Recommended Party:** Gustave  
**Reward:** Colour of Lumina x1, 1 XP  
**NG+ Expert Strategy:** Comfortable. One Overcharge ends it.

☐ Win or lose, continue. Losing the tutorial does not lock anything.

☐ Follow Maelle to the red-and-white tree.

☐ Talk to Maelle. Interact with the rope. Slide down.

### Sophie and Gustave — plaza

You can swap characters with the left stick. Explore the full plaza before the merchant alley.

☐ After the reunion scene, go right around the circle.

☐ Pick up **Chroma** next to the phonograph.

☐ Talk to everyone who will talk. This is lore, not a collectible, but Richard is mandatory.

# ⚠ MISSABLE — Richard

☐ Find **Richard** (long red coat, Sophie's sewing teacher).

☐ Accept **A Uniform for Richard's Son**. You deliver this to Jules at the Festival.

### FROM: plaza, facing the large "Rejoignez L'Expedition" sign

☐ Left side: rummage belongings for Chroma. Play the piano if you want.

☐ Right side: climb the smallest wood stack, then the taller ones. **Chroma** at the top.

☐ Find **Eloise** near the tilting statue.

☐ She wants the punching ball dead in one hit. Attack it until Overcharge is 10. Use **Overcharge**. Do not waste the Overcharge on a normal swing — the ball heals.

☐ Reward: **Colour of Lumina x1**.

☐ Find **Nicolas** the painter (top hat, large canvas) near a horse statue past the flower market, across from where Lune will be playing.

☐ Accept the dodge trial. Dodge Sophie's hits six times in a row.

☐ Reward: **Recoat x1**.

# ⚠ MISSABLE — Trash-can Man

☐ From Nicolas, find the trash can with a **bright rim** near boxes.

☐ Interact. Interact again. Keep going until the text changes and **Trash-can Man** appears.

☐ Take the bribe: **Chroma Catalyst x1**.

# ⚠ MISSABLE — Life Enjoyers

☐ Find the door covered in suitcases near Lune's guitar spot.

☐ You will overhear the "Life Enjoyers."

☐ Interact with that door **33 times**.

☐ They bribe you: **Colour of Lumina x1**.

☐ At the last statue before the harbour (horses), talk to Maelle if she is there.

☐ Left of that statue: **Chroma** in the far corner.

☐ Right of the horse statue: stage with Picto performers. Optional talk with the spectator.

### Lumière Mime

# FROM: horse statue, facing the harbour

☐ The stage with dancing women and fireworks is on the far right.

☐ Walk past the stage into the dark corner.

☐ The Mime stands alone. Interact.

# MIME — Lumière

**Type:** Optional  
**Weakness:** Break bar (yellow). The wall makes damage tiny until Break.  
**Important Attack:** hand-to-hand (two slaps + delayed headbutt). Strange combo (four hammer hits, last slam can delay).  
**Parry/Dodge Notes:** Expert — dodge the 4-hit if you have not memorised the delay.  
**Reward:** **MUSIC RECORD — Lumière**. Achievement A Peculiar Encounter.  
**NG+ Expert Strategy:** Comfortable. Lumière Assault until Break, Overcharge, finish. Retry is free.

# MUSIC RECORD — Lumière

**Area:** Lumière Prologue  
**Nearest Flag / World Landmark:** harbour stage  
**Exact Directions:** past the firework dancers, dark corner  
**How Obtained:** defeat the Mime  
**Missable:** achievement yes; record recoverable in Episode 30 Opera House

☐ When the plaza is empty of pickups, walk Sophie to the harbour.

☐ Confirm the dialogue. This is the point of no return for the plaza.

### Expedition Festival

You start with **1 Festival Token**.

# ⚠ MISSABLE — Festival Token plan (NG+ Expert)

You need **Old Key** and **Weird Pictos**.  
If you already own Gustave's Bun haircut from a previous cycle, **do not buy it**. Keep the third token for a later Baguette weapon (Fextralife: one Baguette weapon per leftover token; full set earliest NG+2).  
If you do not own the Bun, buy it.

### FROM: Festival grounds

☐ **Antoine** (bald, near Lune). Take the quiz.

Antoine answers:

1. When was the Fracture? — **67 years ago**
2. First expedition to the continent? — **Expedition Zero**
3. What is a Lumina? — **Abilities you internalise by using a Pictos in battle enough times**

☐ Reward: Festival Token #2.

☐ **Jules** (small, red coat, glasses, dock stairs). If you have Richard's uniform, he forces it on you.

# OUTFIT — Crimson Uniform (Gustave)

**How:** Richard in the plaza + Jules at the Festival.

☐ Far edge of the Festival, past the stands: talk to **Gustave's apprentices**.

☐ They give **Gustave's Journal**. You write in this at Act I camps. Do not ignore it later.

☐ **Maelle** at the far dock. Duel her.

# BOSS — Maelle (dock)

**Type:** Optional token fight  
**Same moveset as the rooftop.**  
**Reward:** Festival Token #3  
**NG+ Expert Strategy:** Comfortable. You may rematch if you lose. You do **not** get the token if you lose and walk away.
