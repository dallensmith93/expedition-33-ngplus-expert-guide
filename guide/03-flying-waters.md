# Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 — Part 3 — Flying Waters

## EPISODE GOAL

Full Flying Waters clear: both Paint Cages, Journal 68, Demineur quest, Maelle rejoins, optional Bourgeon Skin, every named Picto/weapon on the route. Paint Spikes logged, not broken.

## STARTING POINT

Flying Waters entrance / first interior after the Continent walk from Spring Meadows.

## BEFORE WE START

- Maelle is missing until The Manor beat inside this area. On NG+ she still has her endgame kit when she returns.
- Bourgeon here is optional. If Expert one-shots you, skip and take a Bourgeon Skin from the Episode 7 overworld Bourgeon instead. You only need one Skin for the Small Bourgeon quest.

## WALKTHROUGH

### FROM: Flying Waters entrance

☐ Walk to the central anchor / wreck.

☐ Go **behind** the wreck, back of the starting bowl, red plants.

# PAINT CAGE — Flying Waters 1

**Area:** Flying Waters entrance  
**Nearest Flag:** none yet / first flag after this  
**Reward:** **Chroma Elixir Shard**

### Lock 1
Stand in front of the cage. Turn left. Shoot the faint grey pulse on the wall.

### Lock 2
Keep scanning left. Shoot the tip of the mermaid's tail (Push Square also calls this the whale fin — same prop).

### Lock 3
Turn left again. Behind kelp / the diagonal object. Shoot the grey pulse.

### Cage
Return to the cage and take **Chroma Elixir Shard**.

☐ Continue the main path. Activate **Noco's Hut** when you reach it (or the first named flag in this dungeon — names vary slightly; rest at the hut).

### The Manor / Maelle

☐ Story path into The Manor. Play the Manor sequence. Maelle rejoins.

☐ Exit The Manor back into Flying Waters.

### FROM: Noco's Hut Expedition Flag (after Maelle)

☐ Main path into the next open area.

☐ Maelle suggests the ship. **Do not go through the ship yet.**

☐ Right-hand tunnel.

# NEVRON QUEST — Demineur

☐ Examine the white Demineur. It wants a Mine.

☐ Path to the right of it: round platforms.

☐ Second platform that leads back toward the prior area: **Intact Mine**.

☐ Return the Mine to Demineur.

**Area:** Flying Waters, Coral Cave  
**Nearest Flag:** Coral Cave

**When Available:** now  
**Return Later Required:** No

☐ At the fork where the main path bends right and splits:

☐ **Right:** hole in the ground, red light. Drop.

# PAINT CAGE — Flying Waters 2

**Area:** Coral Cave lower  
**Nearest Flag:** Coral Cave  
**Reward:** **Revive Tint Shard**

Fextralife: ladder on the right of the lantern path → dark area → grapple → three Crulers → cage in the right corner.

### Lock 1
Face the cage. Look right and up. Lock on coral.

### Lock 2
Scan right, slightly behind you (~150°). Lock on the reef.

### Lock 3
From lock 2, walk right. Shoot the floating mine — the lock is on/behind it.

### Cage
**Revive Tint Shard**.

☐ Climb back to the fork.

☐ **Left** path (if you have not already): confirm Journal 68 if you skipped it.

☐ Clear the Cruler / coral enemies. Pick up every Colour of Lumina on the floor as you pass.

# NEW ENEMY — Cruler

# BOSS — Bourgeon (Flying Waters)

**Type:** Optional  
**Reward:** **Bourgeon Skin** (quest item, need one for Episode 7)  
**NG+ Expert Strategy:** Strong Build Recommended if you do not have Cheater active. It eats a character — do not let Gustave get swallowed first if he is your Overcharge.  
**If you fail:** leave. Episode 7 overworld Bourgeon drops another Skin.

# MONOCO SKILL AVAILABLE

**Enemy:** Bourgeon  
**Skill:** (Bourgeon skill — VERIFY IN GAME exact name; PowerPyx lists Bourgeon encounters for the Skin, not a unique trophy skill)  
**Return:** Episode 13+

### FROM: Lumieran Streets Expedition Flag

☐ Activate. Full street sweep. Open every side alley.

☐ Shoot Paint Cage-style glowing bits only if they are locks — there is no third cage here.

**Type:** Story  
**Nearest Flag:** Flower Field
