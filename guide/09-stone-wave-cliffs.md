# BOSS — Lampmaster

**Type:** Story  
**Important Mechanics:** it lights lanterns. **Shoot the lanterns in the same order** on your turn or it gets extra attacks.  
**Dangerous Attacks:** multi-lamp beams, blight on the rematch (not always on this fight).  
**Parry/Dodge Notes:** learn the slam / swipe. Expert does not forgive a full string.  
**Recommended Party:** Maelle, Lune, Sciel  
**Reward:** Act I ends. Florrie recovered. **Swim unlocks after the scenes.**  
**NG+ Expert Strategy:** Strong Build Recommended. Do the lanterns every time. Do not greed a nuke through an extra lamp turn.

☐ Watch the Act I ending scenes. Gustave's story beat occurs here.
