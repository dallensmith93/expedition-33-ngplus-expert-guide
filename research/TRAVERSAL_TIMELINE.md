# TRAVERSAL TIMELINE — Esquie and related unlocks

Current version: 1.5.5. NG+ Expert: **all Esquie abilities reset**. Paint Break also resets.

---

## 1. Story join — Esquie's Nest (Episode 6)

**When:** After Gestral Village tournament. Golgra gives the password. Francois fight. Esquie joins.

**What he can do immediately:**
- Mount on the Continent (overworld).
- Charge / smash **overworld rock barriers** (not Paint Spikes).
- Reach previously blocked land paths: Yellow Harvest, Stone Wave Cliffs approach, Twilight Quarry, Ancient Gestral City, Lost Woods (via Yellow Harvest exit), extra Gestral Beaches, Abbest Cave access improvements.

**Cannot yet:** swim, break coral, fly, dive, break Paint Spikes.

### NEWLY ACCESSIBLE CONTENT (mount / smash rocks)

#### New Areas
- Yellow Harvest + Chromatic boss path + Lost Woods exit
- Twilight Quarry
- Ancient Gestral City
- Stone Wave Cliffs approach (story)
- Extra Gestral Beach (obstacle course if not done)
- Overworld Bourgeon east of Stone Wave Cliffs ramp

#### Weapons
- Gaulteram / Abysseram from the Stone Wave Cliffs Bourgeon pack (Gustave/Verso)
- Area drops inside Yellow Harvest

#### Pictos
- Augmented Counter I from that Bourgeon pack
- Yellow Harvest / overworld pictos found during Episodes 7–8

#### Journals
- Expedition 62 (north of Gestral Village, behind smashable rocks)
- Expedition 38, 44, 59 (Yellow Harvest)

#### Music Records
- Un 33 Décembre à Lumière (Twilight Quarry)
- Lights of the Past (Ancient Gestral City)
- Aline's Glasshouse (Lost Woods)

#### Lost Gestrals
- **#1** already grabbed at Esquie's Nest portal (Episode 6)
- **#2** east of Stone Wave Cliffs ramp (Episode 7 or 9)

#### Paint Cages / Paint Spikes
- None new from mount alone. Paint Spikes remain **RETURN LATER**.

#### Merchants / Quests / Bosses
- Overworld Bourgeon + Volester + Gault
- Yellow Harvest Chromatic / optional bosses
- Small Bourgeon Nevron quest can be finished if you already have a Bourgeon Skin

**Routed into:** Episodes 7, 8, 9.

## 2. Swim — Florrie (end of Episode 9 / start of Episode 10)

**When:** After Lampmaster at Stone Wave Cliffs. Esquie recovers Florrie. Act I ends / Act II begins.

**What it opens:** open water, islands, White Tree, The Meadows, Blades' Graveyard, Boat Graveyard, extra Gestral Beach (volley), first Painting Workshop visit, many swim-only journals/records.

**Cannot yet:** coral channels, flying islands, underwater whirlpools.

### NEWLY ACCESSIBLE CONTENT (swim)

#### New Areas
- White Tree
- The Meadows
- Blades' Graveyard
- Boat Graveyard
- Second Gestral Beach (raft volley)
- Painting Workshop (visit 1)
- Coastal approaches that do **not** require coral smash
- Sinister Cave / some eastern water only if no coral blocks the lane — **VERIFY IN GAME** for exact coral walls; if blocked, defer to Episode 15

#### Journals
- Expedition 64 (shore south of The Meadows)
- Expedition 36 (White Tree, with the record)

#### Music Records
- Our Painted Family (The Meadows)
- Honeymoon in Lumière (White Tree)
- Révérance (Blades' Graveyard)
- Alicia's Birthday Party (Boat Graveyard)

#### Lost Gestrals
- **#3** White Tree island (southwest of portal, by vase / tall grass)

#### Other
- Swim-only Colour of Lumina / catalyst pickups (not listed exhaustively — farmable/generic)

**Routed into:** Episode 10 first, leftovers into Episode 11 world-map extras.

## 3. Paint Break — Sastro, 4 Lost Gestrals (end of Episode 11 / Episode 12)

**When:** After Lost Gestrals 1–4 are found and you speak to Sastro at camp.

**The four we use:**
1. Esquie's Nest portal
2. East of Stone Wave Cliffs ramp
3. White Tree island (swim)
4. Between Forgotten Battlefield exit and Monoco's Station (green-leaf pair)

**What it opens:** every blue Paint Spike in previously visited areas.

**Cannot yet:** coral, fly, dive.

### NEWLY ACCESSIBLE CONTENT (Paint Break)

Act I/early Act II spikes in:
- Spring Meadows (Aegis Revival behind Jar, others)
- Flying Waters
- Ancient Sanctuary / Gestral Village
- Esquie's Nest
- Yellow Harvest
- Stone Wave Cliffs
- Forgotten Battlefield
- Camp spike next to Sastro (Colour of Lumina x1)

**Routed into:** Episode 12 dedicated backtrack. Later spikes cleared on first visit once the ability is owned.

**What it opens:** coral-gated islands and channels around Old Lumière, Coastal Cave beach, red-tree Mime island, Falling Leaves, Sirène/Visages sea lanes, extra merchants.

- Falling Leaves
- Western corridor beaches
- Sirène / Visages approaches
- Sinister Cave if previously coral-blocked
- Extra Gestral Beach (race)
- Isle merchants (Rederi — Robe de Jour)

#### Journals
- Expedition 40 (northwest shop island)
- Expedition 35, 49 (Falling Leaves)
- Expedition 43 (east island near Chromatic Reaper Cultist)

#### Music Records
- Alicia (Falling Leaves Manor door)
- Until Next Life (Falling Leaves after Scavenger)
- Robe de Jour (Rederi, south of Sirène)

#### Lost Gestrals

#### Bosses
- Falling Leaves Scavenger
- Coastal Cave packs (Stalact / Ballet / Sapling)

**Routed into:** Episode 15, then 16–17 naturally.

## 6. Dive — Urrie (Episode 25)

**When:** Esquie relationship **Level 6** at camp in Act III. Triggers Francois 1v1. Reward: Urrie + underwater dive.

**Earliest sensible moment:** first Act III camp after flight, once Esquie ranks 1–5 from Act II are already done. This guide finishes Esquie 1–5 during Acts I–II camps, then dumps ranks 5→7 at the Episode 21 camp / Episode 25.

**What it opens:**
- Glowing blue whirlpools on the Continent (catalysts / Colour of Lumina)
- **Verso's Drafts** portal east of Lumière (Thank You Update) — official 1.5.0 requirement
- Renoir's Drafts water interaction south between Visages and Sirène (the dive/spot to enter)

### NEWLY ACCESSIBLE CONTENT (dive)

#### New Areas
- Verso's Drafts + Root of All Evil
- Renoir's Drafts / The Abyss (if the entrance is the glowing water spot)
- All whirlpool material nodes

#### Weapons / Pictos / Cosmetics
- Full Verso's Drafts list (see `THANK_YOU_UPDATE.md`)
- Simon rewards (Simoso, Journal Simon)
- Endless Light record

**Routed into:** Episode 25 (dive + whirlpools + Renoir's Drafts start if appropriate), Episodes 26–27 (Verso's Drafts), Episode 24/28 (Simon if not already done).

## 7. Fast travel Continent-from-any-flag

Resets in NG+. Unlocks again through story/camp progression (same as first play). Not a collectible. Use it as soon as the game offers it.

## What does NOT require a new Esquie ability

- Paint Cages (aim + shoot locks)
- Manor doors (area-specific)
- Relationship ranks (camp + a few destination quests)
- Endless Tower portraits (need the **original boss killed**, not a new traversal)
