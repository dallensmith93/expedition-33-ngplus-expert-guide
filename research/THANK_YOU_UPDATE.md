### Osquio (Superboss Build Recommended)

- No elemental weakness. Shoot the head.
- If he takes a big chunk early he spins the belt buckle and heals **once**.
- Long HP fight. Maelle Defenceless / Virtuose + Lune/Verso follow-ups.
- 1.5.1 moved the red lifebuoy slightly to make a quest easier — if a cake/quest NPC path looks wrong versus a pre-1.5.1 video, trust the current geometry.

## Endless Tower — Thank You

**Access:** fly to the giant doorway, west/northwest of Gestral Village / Yellow Harvest.

### Base tower (pre-1.5.0, still required)

- 11 stages × 3 trials = 33 fights.
- Record **Clea** + Outfit Clair (Verso) + Perfect catalyst on Stage 11 Trial 3 (Painted Love).
- Talk to Fading Woman afterward: **Ponytail (Maelle)**.
- 1.5.0 extra: **Visages outfit (Verso)** under the rightmost staircase, north to a glowing red pickup.

### Four new portrait bosses (behind / beside the main canvas)

Unlock after the original is dead:

| Portrait | Original required | Reward highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Chromatic Lampmaster | Lampmaster (Stone Wave Cliffs / Workshop rematch) | First Life Picto |
| Duollistes | Dualliste (Forgotten Battlefield) | Duollison (Sciel) |
| Clea Unleashed | Clea (Flying Manor) | Cleim (Lune), Clea's Death |
| Simon the Divergent Star | Simon (The Abyss) | Simon haircut for every party member |

Clea Unleashed path (Game8): stairs past the canvas → grapple → right to her portrait. She does not let you take a turn until a full combo is dodged/parried. Solo carry recommended so "creatures of the deep" cannot delete the DPS.

### Recommended order vs the rest of Act III

1. Early tower Stages 1–7 while flying (Episode 22/23) for pictos/haircuts
2. Flying Manor Clea (Episode 24)
3. Dive + Simon (Episode 25/27)
4. Tower Stages 8–11 (Episode 28)
5. All four portraits (Episode 28)
