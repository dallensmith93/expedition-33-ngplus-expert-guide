State key:
- **E##** = completed during that episode
- **RL→E##** = return later, scheduled
- **FINALE** = only possible inside / after the Lumière finale (Episode 30)
- **REPEAT** = farmable / not required for 100%

Counts below are the trophy/meaningful finite sets. Pictos and weapons are larger than trophy lists; every unique named pick-up is still routed in the episode files. If a Picto is only a random overworld drop, it is REPEAT.

## Monoco skills (44 trophy + Thank You extras)

PowerPyx trophy list is 44. Keep Monoco in the party for every new enemy type. Episode 13 does the Act I/II overworld skill sweep. Episode 24 Flying Manor covers Duallist Storm + Lampmaster Light if still missing. Episode 26–27 covers Drafts enemies (Cannaro weapon is separate from a skill). Episode 28 tower can finish any leftover skill. Episode 30 covers Aberration Light + Creation Void.

If Feet Collection pops before Lumière, the only remaining skills are the two Lumière enemies.
