# ROUTE DECISIONS

Fixed constraint: exactly 30 public episode titles. Content may move; titles may not.

Core rule: **everything reasonably possible is finished before the Lumière finale portal.** Episode 29 proves it. Episode 30 is the finale.

---

## Decision 1 — Paint Break sits in Episode 12, not after Monoco

PowerPyx awards Paint Break at the Monoco's Station camp because that is when they happen to have Gestral #4 and a camp trigger.

**Our four Gestrals before Monoco's Station:**
1. Esquie's Nest portal — Episode 6
2. Stone Wave Cliffs east — Episode 7
3. White Tree — Episode 10
4. Green-leaf trees between Forgotten Battlefield and Monoco's Station — Episode 11 exit

Sastro exists from Gestral #1. After Gestral #4 we camp immediately, take Paint Break, then Episode 12 backtracks every Act I / early Act II spike. Episode 13 is Monoco.

If a camp trigger refuses to fire before Monoco joins: **VERIFY IN GAME**. Sleep / leave / re-enter camp. If Sastro still will not hand Paint Break, enter Monoco's Station just far enough to force the camp, take the ability, then do Episode 12 before continuing Monoco's story.

## Decision 2 — Sirène before Visages

Episode titles force 16 then 17. Both Axons are optional-order in the real game.

**First Axon = Sirène (Episode 16).**  
Immediately after: camp → **Check up on the others** → missable **Lettre à Maelle**.

Visages is Episode 17. Monolith is 18–20.

## Decision 3 — Swim content is Episode 10, not a post-Forgotten-Battlefield dump

Episode 10 title is *Exploring the Waters*. Swim unlocks at the end of Episode 9. We do White Tree / Meadows / Blades / Boat Graveyard / volley beach / Painting Workshop 1 **before** Forgotten Battlefield.

Hard swim-adjacent areas that are clearly over-levelled even on NG+ (if any) get a **Strong Build** tag, not a deferral, unless a coral wall actually blocks them.

## Decision 4 — Coral content is Episode 15

Episode 15 is *Act II Side-Area Sweep*. Coral unlocks at the end of Old Lumière (Episode 14). Episode 15 is Falling Leaves, Coastal Cave, red Mime island, leftover swim/coral merchants, Sinister Cave, Gestral race, Rederi.

Sirène / Visages stay in 16–17 so the titles stay honest.

## Decision 5 — Flight dump is 21–24, not "postgame"

PowerPyx and many guides call flying areas "endgame / after the story." That is convenience, not a lock.

Confirmed available after Paintress, before Lumière:
- The Reacher, Sirène's Dress, Sacred River (relationship 6–7)
- Frozen Hearts, Endless Tower 33 trials, Flying Manor / Clea
- Renoir's Drafts / Simon (dive)
- Sprong, Serpenphare, Grosse Tête, Chromatic Petank
- All remaining Lost Gestrals, journals 34–49 except Lumière-only 39 and finale-auto 26/Our Drafts Collide
- Thank You Verso's Drafts (dive)
- Thank You Endless Tower portraits (after originals)

We do them in 21–28.

## Decision 6 — Dive is Episode 25, Verso's Drafts is 26–27

1.5.0: Verso's Drafts is next to Lumière and **requires Esquie's underwater ability**. It does **not** require entering the Lumière finale instance.

Episode 21 camps Esquie to rank 6 as soon as Act III relationships unlock. If the game gates rank 6 behind leaving camp once (PowerPyx), we do that leave/return in Episode 21, then dive content can start late 21 / 25.

Episode 25: Francois 1v1 if not done, whirlpools, Renoir's Drafts entrance, Endless Light, Simon if the party is ready (otherwise Simon stays late 27 / 28).

Episode 26: Verso's Drafts natural first clear.  
Episode 27: leftover Drafts + Osquio + Licornapieds.  
Episode 28: Endless Tower remaining trials + TY portraits.
