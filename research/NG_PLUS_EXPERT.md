# NG+ EXPERT — what this run actually is

This walkthrough assumes you already finished the game once (or more) and started **New Game+** with **Expert** difficulty.

---

## What carries over

Confirmed across Fextralife, GameRant, ScreenRant:

- Character levels, attributes, unspent skill points
- All unlocked skills and Gradient Attacks
- All party members except the story-gated join order (they become usable again as the story allows; NG+ does **not** let Gustave stay after Old Lumière)
- All weapons, including max-upgrade progress
- All Pictos and learned Luminas
- Tint upgrades / Chroma Elixir upgrades
- Colour of Lumina bank
- Recoats, catalysts in inventory
- All outfits and hairstyles
- Gustave can wear Verso/Renoir-style cosmetics that were unlocked on Verso

## What resets (you must collect again)

- Story flags and area completion
- Side quests (Nevron quests, Gestral games, Sastro)
- Quest items (Old Key, Festival Tokens, Bourgeon Skin, Resin, Rock Crystals, Wooden Boards, Workshop Beast items, Eternal Ice, Manor Family Canvas)
- Music Records
- Expedition Journals
- Lost Gestrals
- Paint Break (and every Paint Spike)
- Esquie traversal rocks
- Relationship levels (Gradient Attacks already earned stay)
- Instant Continent travel from any flag until re-unlocked

## What changes in the world

- Enemies have far more HP and damage. Expert on top of NG+ means unparried hits delete people even if you are level 70–99.
- Low-tier catalysts in the world are often replaced by **Grandiose Chroma Catalyst** (and later Perfect).
- Re-looting a Picto you already own: either a **free upgrade level** or conversion to Chroma if that upgrade is already maxed.
- Re-looting a weapon you already own: free upgrade / chroma conversion. Still pick them up.
- Festival Tokens: 3 exist in the Prologue. Old Key and Weird Pictos still matter because quest items reset. The Bun haircut is already owned — **save that token** if you want a Baguette weapon later. Earliest all-character Baguette set is NG+2 (Fextralife).
- 1.5.0: Old Key also appears in the final Lumière area. We still buy it in the Prologue.

## Expert-specific rules used in this guide

Do **not** follow first-play level recommendations.

Difficulty tags used before bosses:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Comfortable | NG+ kit already trivialises it if you can parry the opener |
| Strong Build Recommended | Bring a real rotation (Burn/Mark/Break). Don't meme it. |
| Optimized Build Recommended | Painted Power + Cheater-level setup, or you will time-out / get clipped |
| Endgame Build Recommended | Flying Manor / tower mid-late / Verso's Drafts chromatics |
| Superboss Build Recommended | Simon, Clea, Osquio, TY Endless Tower portraits |

## Build philosophy (not repeated every five minutes)

**Painted Power** (Paintress reward) must go on everyone as a Lumina the moment Act III starts. Without it you cap at 9,999.

**Cheater** (Sprong) is the single biggest NG+ Expert quality-of-life Picto. This guide kills Sprong in Episode 21/22 as soon as flight is up, unless you already own Cheater from the previous cycle — you do, because Pictos carry over. **If Cheater already sits in your Lumina list, do not wait.** Equip it from Episode 1.

That is the entire NG+ Expert secret: you already have the broken toys. The walkthrough still visits Sprong so the Picto can upgrade / convert, and so the world-boss flag is cleared.

## Party reality on NG+

- Prologue / Act I: Gustave, Lune, Maelle, then Sciel. Verso weapons you carried over cannot show their Level Upgrade bonuses on Gustave until Verso exists again.
- After Old Lumière: Verso replaces Gustave permanently. Gustave cosmetics remain in the wardrobe.
- Keep Monoco in the active trio whenever a **new enemy type** is on the next screen. Skills do not reset; you only need new enemy types (Thank You enemies, anything you skipped).

## Expert combat notes that apply everywhere

- Dodge windows are tighter than Expedition. If a first-play guide says "you can facetank this," ignore it.
- First Strike + Cheater on the carry (usually Maelle or Verso) ends 90% of overworld packs before they act.
- Mimes still wall. Break the yellow bar. NG+ HP makes "slow burn" Mimes actually dangerous — Break then dump.
- Petanks still flee. Have a rush / First Strike plan.
- Chromatics in Act I are Comfortable if you already have Painted Power from the previous cycle. You do.

## NG+ Expert use of Festival Tokens (Episode 1)

1. **Colette — Old Key.** Mandatory. Quest item reset.
2. **Tom — Weird Pictos.** Mandatory. Needed later in Gestral Village for Augmented Aim.
3. **Amandine — Bun haircut.** Skip if you already own it; keep the token for a later Baguette weapon. If you do not own the haircut, buy it.

Antoine quiz + Maelle dock duel still give the extra tokens.
